Highlights
- Every N >= 2 is a sum a+b with Omega(ab) <= 32, improving the bound 33
- The explicit sieve bound is proved with the dimension condition at one level
- Per-interval dimension constants and pre-sieving by 3 and 5 close the odd case
- The finite range is certified in ball arithmetic over 117,415 intervals
- The certificate code is public and reproduces bit for bit
